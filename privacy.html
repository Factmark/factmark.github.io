Privacy

Last updated 30 September 2026. This covers the Factmark waitlist on this website.

What we collect

Your email address, and your part of London if you choose to tell us. We also note when you joined.

Why

Only to email you when Factmark is ready to try, and to know which parts of London people want next. You give your consent by ticking the box on the form. We won’t send marketing, and we won’t share, sell or rent your details.

Where it’s kept

In a private database run by Supabase in London. Only the Factmark team can read it. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts come from Google Fonts; like any website host, they may log your IP address when you visit.

How long

Until Factmark launches in your area and we’ve told you, or until you ask us to remove you, whichever comes first.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or withdraw consent, at any time by emailing factmark.hello@outlook.com. Every email we send will also include a way to unsubscribe. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Who we are

Factmark is being built in London by Rowan Grundy. Contact: factmark.hello@outlook.com.
