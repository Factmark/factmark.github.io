Questions

Good to know

Where does it work?

We’re starting around Clapham Common in south London, with more of London to follow. Tell us your part of London when you join and we’ll let you know when it reaches you.

Is it free?

Walking alerts will be free. Some extras may become part of a paid plan later, and we’ll always tell you before anything costs money.

Are the stories true?

Yes. Every fact is checked against a source, such as Historic England, local history societies and museum archives, before it’s published. When sources disagree, the story says so.

What about my location?

Your location is used on your phone to find stories near you. It isn’t shared or sold.

Which phones?

iPhone first. Android will follow.
